OKN Studio is an English-language visual stimulus and observation utility for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

Start a display

In Studio, choose a direction, speed and duration. Tap Quick Start to begin, or Open full screen to prepare before starting. Pause and Close are available in the display. A locked display can always be paused.

Adjust targets and timing

Open Advanced controls for patterns, target width, colors, contrast, fixation and the metronome. Session sequences lets you build ordered display and rest steps. Save frequently used settings as presets and select up to three favorites in Library.

Record and review

Start the front camera from the display after obtaining consent. Recording is silent. Use Mark to add a time marker. In Library, open a video to scrub, slow playback or step through frames where the decoder supports it. Camera recording stops when the display is paused, rotated, closed or interrupted.

Notes and PDF reports

Open Library → Sessions → a session. Add an optional client code and observation note, then prepare and share a PDF report. In the Amsler tool, prepare an image and attach it to a session if needed. Reports contain your observations; the app does not interpret them.

Understand the units

In flat mode, speed is measured in screen points per second and target width in screen points. These are not physical millimeters or calibrated visual degrees. Amsler scale can be adjusted with a physical ruler, but this does not establish clinical accuracy.

Settings and backups

Choose dark, light or system appearance, left-handed controls and an optional countdown in Settings. Settings backup exports preferences, presets, sequences and custom target images. It does not export session records or videos. Share those separately and store any sensitive information appropriately.

Troubleshooting

If recording is unavailable, allow camera access in iOS Settings and check free storage. For metronome sound, check device volume and the current audio output. Reminder delivery depends on notification permission and iOS notification settings. If a backup fails to import, use a complete, unmodified OKN Studio settings backup.

Purchases

OKN Studio is a one-time paid download with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Download availability and regional pricing are displayed by the App Store. Apple handles purchases and refund requests through Report a Problem.

Professional use

The app does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, quantify nystagmus or perform automated eye tracking. Experimental panorama/headset modes are not medically calibrated VR. Professionals must independently assess suitability, device performance and their intended use. Stop if discomfort occurs.
