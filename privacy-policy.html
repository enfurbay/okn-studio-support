Privacy

Your data stays
under your control.

Effective September 30, 2026. This policy describes OKN Studio for iPhone and iPad, developed by Enes Furkan Bayram.

Data collection

OKN Studio does not send app usage, session records, recordings or identifiers to the developer. It has no account system, advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or developer-operated cloud backend. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

Information on your device

Your preferences, presets, session sequences, optional client codes, observation notes, imported target images, Amsler observation images, silent videos and video time markers are stored locally. Avoid entering identifying information that is unnecessary for your work. You are responsible for appropriate consent and handling of any information you record about another person.

Camera, photos and device motion

The front camera is used only when you explicitly start a recording and grant permission. Recordings do not include microphone audio. There is no face identification, biometric recognition or automated eye tracking. The system photo picker lets you choose a target image without granting unrestricted photo-library access. Optional immersive previews use device motion locally.

Notifications and reviews

Reminders are optional local notifications on the schedule you select. They do not use a remote push-notification server. Review requests use Apple's system interface. You can disable reminders and review requests in Settings.

Exports, backups and deletion

Sharing opens the system share sheet; you select the destination. An export sent to another app or service is governed by that destination's privacy practices. Settings backups are readable JSON files and may contain your custom target images. They include preferences, presets and sequences, but do not include session notes, observation images or videos. iOS device backups may separately contain app data according to your Apple backup settings.

You can delete saved sessions, presets and recordings from the app. Deleting the app removes its local data, although copies in device backups, Files or destinations you previously shared to may remain. Manage those copies in the relevant service or device settings.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the contents you choose to send, and uses them to answer your request. Do not send patient records or sensitive clinical images. Support correspondence is kept only as necessary to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you may request its deletion.

Apple services and external links

Purchases, system permissions, device backups and App Store reviews are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's policies. External reference and support links open websites governed by their own policies.

This website

This support website uses GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and protect its hosting service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

OKN Studio is intended for professional use. Illustrated targets may be used under appropriate adult supervision; the app does not create child accounts or collect children's information for the developer.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if our privacy practices change. Contact Enes Furkan Bayram at enfurbay@gmail.com for privacy questions or support-data deletion requests.
